# Ontario Insurer Logo Assets & Attribution (INS-64 Section 13)

This registry records the asset source, brand representations, and nominative fair use basis for insurer brand marks displayed on the InsurCheck Insurer Reviews directory.

## Permission & Legal Basis
* **Nominative Fair Use**: Brand logos are displayed strictly for editorial identification and comparison of licensed Ontario property & casualty insurance carriers.
* **Non-Endorsement**: Display of carrier brand marks does not imply affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement by InsurCheck or any insurer.
* **Proportions & Integrity**: Official colors, geometries, and typography are preserved without distortion or derogatory alteration.
* **Fallback Protection**: Should an asset fail to load or permission be contested, the platform automatically falls back to neutral initials placeholders.
* **Accessibility**: Logos include `aria-hidden="true"` and empty `alt=""` attributes to prevent redundant announcements to screen-reader users, as the adjacent text independently names the carrier.
